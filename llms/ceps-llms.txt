# CEPS (Centre for European Policy Studies)

> Founded in Brussels in 1983, CEPS is a leading independent think tank and forum for debate on
> EU affairs. It conducts evidence-based policy research on European and global issues — the Data
> Governance Act, the Data Act, the AI Act, climate, energy, migration, trade, defence and
> financial markets — and disseminates it through publications, events, task forces and podcasts.
> CEPS is a research producer, not a software vendor: it publishes no API, no SDK and no
> developer program.
